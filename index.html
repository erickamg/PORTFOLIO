StudyMate

A student productivity web app that keeps academics and money in one place. A live task dashboard tracks total, completed, pending, and overdue tasks, with quick add, edit, and mark-as-done actions. A Deadlines calendar and Subjects view keep coursework organized by class, while a built-in Finance module handles budget tracking right alongside the workload — all tied together under a personal Profile.

Neal's Chicken

A food delivery web application designed to make online food ordering more convenient by providing customers with a seamless process, from browsing meals to tracking their orders in real time for a restaurant brand. The project includes features such as menu browsing, user authentication, checkout, promotions, order tracking, and order history, aiming to create an efficient and user-friendly system that improves the overall customer experience.

Edd's Revenue Tracker

A role-based revenue tracking system built for Edd's Ngohiong, a small food business, replacing manual spreadsheet tracking with real-time digital monitoring. Cashiers log transactions as they happen, while Admins get a full dashboard with daily, weekly, and monthly revenue summaries, visualized through trend charts. The system also includes secure user management, with hashed passwords, role-based access control, and account activity controls for Admins.

  • MySQL
